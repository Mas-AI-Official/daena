1. Prohibited Uses

You may not use the Service, directly or indirectly, to engage in or facilitate any of the following activities:

1.1 Illegal Activity

  • Any activity that violates any applicable local, provincial, national, or international law or regulation;
  • Facilitating or promoting illegal transactions, money laundering, or terrorist financing;
  • Tax evasion or financial fraud;
  • Violating export control laws, trade sanctions, or embargoes;
  • Any activity that would subject MAS-AI to criminal or civil liability.

1.2 Harmful and Abusive Content

  • Generating, distributing, or facilitating child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, including AI-generated imagery or text depicting minors in sexual situations;
  • Generating content that promotes, glorifies, or incites violence against any individual or group;
  • Harassment, bullying, intimidation, stalking, or threatening any individual;
  • Generating or distributing non-consensual intimate imagery (including AI-generated "deepfakes");
  • Content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic;
  • Content designed to exploit, harm, or endanger minors in any way;
  • Generating content that constitutes defamation, libel, or slander.

1.3 Fraud and Deception

  • Impersonating any person, entity, or organization, including MAS-AI employees;
  • Creating fraudulent content for phishing, scams, social engineering, or identity theft;
  • Generating misleading or deceptive content intended to manipulate public opinion, elections, or financial markets;
  • Creating fake reviews, testimonials, endorsements, or credentials;
  • Using AI Output to fabricate false evidence, documents, or records;
  • Misrepresenting AI-generated content as human-created in contexts where such distinction is material or legally required.

1.4 Malicious Technical Activity

  • Generating, developing, or distributing malware, viruses, ransomware, trojans, worms, or any other malicious software;
  • Using the Service to identify or exploit vulnerabilities in systems you do not own or have authorization to test;
  • Generating code designed to facilitate unauthorized access to computer systems or networks;
  • Using the Service for distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks or preparation thereof;
  • Generating spam, unsolicited communications, or content for mass exploitation campaigns;
  • Attempting to probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service or any related system or network.

1.5 Platform Integrity Violations

  • Attempting to circumvent, disable, or interfere with any security features of the Service, including but not limited to SecurityGate, governance controls, audit trails, rate limits, or access restrictions;
  • Attempting to disable, bypass, or tamper with the Service's AI governance pipeline;
  • Reverse engineering, decompiling, disassembling, or otherwise attempting to discover the source code, algorithms, or architecture of the Service;
  • Scraping, crawling, or using automated tools to extract data from the Service except through officially provided APIs;
  • Creating accounts by automated means or under false or fraudulent pretenses;
  • Sharing account credentials or allowing unauthorized third-party access to your account;
  • Using the Service to develop, train, or improve a competing AI product or service;
  • Interfering with or disrupting the Service or servers or networks connected to the Service;
  • Removing, altering, or obscuring any proprietary notices, labels, or markings on the Service.

1.6 Regulated and High-Risk Uses

The Service is not designed, intended, or authorized for use in the following high-risk contexts without explicit written approval from MAS-AI:

  • Medical diagnosis, treatment planning, or clinical decision-making;
  • Legal advice or the practice of law;
  • Financial advice, investment decisions, or securities trading;
  • Operation or control of critical infrastructure (power grids, water systems, transportation);
  • Autonomous weapons systems or military decision-making;
  • Decisions that materially affect a person's civil rights, employment, housing, credit, insurance, or education;
  • Law enforcement profiling, surveillance, or predictive policing;
  • Real-time safety-critical systems where AI failure could cause injury or death.

3. Automated Access Restrictions

Automated access to the Service is subject to the following restrictions:

  • API Access Only: Automated access must use our officially provided APIs. Direct scraping, crawling, or automated interaction with the web interface is prohibited;
  • Authentication Required: All automated access must use valid API keys or authentication tokens associated with a registered account;
  • Bot Identification: Automated systems must identify themselves via appropriate User-Agent headers;
  • Rate Limit Compliance: Automated systems must respect rate limits and implement exponential backoff on rate-limit responses;
  • No Credential Harvesting: Automated tools must not attempt to harvest, collect, or enumerate user credentials or account information;
  • No Automated Account Creation: Creating accounts through automated means is prohibited;
  • Data Extraction Limits: Automated bulk extraction of data, AI Output, or Service content beyond your normal use is prohibited;
  • Training Data Prohibition: Using automated access to collect data for training competing AI models is strictly prohibited.
