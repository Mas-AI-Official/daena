# Research Skill

Conduct thorough research by gathering information from multiple sources and synthesizing findings.

## When to Use

- User asks to research a topic in depth
- Comparing technologies, products, or approaches
- Competitive analysis
- Market research or trend analysis
- Academic or technical literature review

## Research Process

1. **Define scope**: Clarify what exactly needs to be researched and what the output should be
2. **Gather sources**: Use web search, documentation, and available tools to collect information
3. **Cross-reference**: Verify facts across multiple sources
4. **Synthesize**: Combine findings into a coherent analysis
5. **Cite sources**: Always link to original sources

## Output Format

Structure research as:
- Executive summary (2-3 sentences)
- Key findings (bulleted list)
- Detailed analysis (sections by subtopic)
- Sources (linked)
- Recommendations (if applicable)

## Quality Checks

- Are claims backed by sources?
- Are multiple perspectives represented?
- Is the information current (check dates)?
- Are limitations and uncertainties noted?
