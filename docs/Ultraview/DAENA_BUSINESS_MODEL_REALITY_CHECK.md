# Daena Business Model Reality Check

Date: 2026-04-30

## External Signals Checked

- Deloitte predicts SaaS will see AI-agent experimentation and restructuring in 2026, but not a complete agent replacement of enterprise applications in 2026: https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/technology/technology-media-and-telecom-predictions/2026/saas-ai-agents.html
- Zapier's 2026 enterprise survey reports strong planned AI-agent investment, but also fragmented build choices across cloud, open-source, platforms, and orchestration frameworks: https://zapier.com/blog/ai-agents-survey
- Palantir describes AIP as governed generative AI connected to operations, agents, automations, model management, evaluations, and data governance: https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/platform-overview/aip-capabilities/
- Palantir's own AIP overview frames AIP with Foundry and Apollo as an operating system for AI-driven products: https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/aip/overview/
- Palantir public investor/news material describes AIP Bootcamps as hands-on acceleration from zero to use case: https://palantir2020ipo.q4web.com/news-details/2024/Palantir-to-Unveil-New-Customers-at-Oversubscribed-AIPCon/default.aspx

## Brutal Verdict

Pure SaaS is not Daena's strongest wedge right now.

The current UI has too many half-real pages for a self-serve SaaS buyer. A self-serve buyer needs install, connect, run, trust, and pay in one clean path. Daena currently has strong ingredients but inconsistent truth surfaces. Selling this as a polished SaaS today would create churn and credibility damage.

Daena is stronger as an AI-enabled operating partner / deployment service first:

- founder-led diagnosis;
- governed runtime integration;
- MCP/provider/local model cleanup;
- security/compliance workflows;
- automation execution with audit;
- monthly operating retainer.

The product should become the internal operating console for that service before it becomes pure SaaS.

## Recommended Wedge

High-ticket governed AI operations deployment for security-conscious teams:

> "In 30 days, we turn your messy AI tools, local models, SaaS apps, docs, and workflows into one governed operating layer with audit trails, approval gates, and working automations."

Initial ICP:

- small to mid-market companies already paying for multiple AI tools;
- agencies/consultancies drowning in manual ops;
- security/compliance-sensitive teams that cannot use open-loop agents;
- founder-led teams that want automation but need control.

## What To Remove From UI For This Path

- Production-looking billing/quota until it is real.
- Huge plugin marketplace cards.
- Overdesigned governance tier UI.
- Any "connected/imported/healthy" label not backed by a test.
- Any page that cannot save, execute, audit, or explain why it cannot.

## What To Emphasize

- Runtime & Connections truth.
- Workstreams and tasks.
- Approvals and audit log.
- Security Ops and Scan Scope.
- Files and knowledge status.
- Chat/history.
- Founder/company context.

## 30-Day Revenue Plan

1. Package one offer: `AI Ops Control Room Buildout`.
2. Price: high-ticket setup plus monthly retainer, not $29 self-serve SaaS.
3. Promise: governed runtime integration, 3-5 automations live, audit trail, policy gates, knowledge sync status.
4. Use Daena internally during delivery; do not pretend it is turnkey SaaS.
5. Convert repeated delivery steps into productized onboarding.

## Investor Narrative Adjustment

Do not pitch "another AI SaaS dashboard." Pitch:

- governed orchestration over any runtime;
- operating partner wedge now;
- SaaS platform later;
- service delivery creates workflow data, integration playbooks, and proof;
- product becomes the deployment console for a repeatable AI operating system.
